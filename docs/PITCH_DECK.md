# ScanGo
**Khách tự order = tiết kiệm 5M/tháng**

Self-Service QR Ordering for Vietnamese Coffee & Tea Shops

---

## The Problem

**Vietnamese coffee/tea shops (5-15 tables) face 3 pain points:**

1. **Labor dependency** — 5M/month cashier, hard to find/retain staff
2. **Peak hour bottleneck** — customers queue 2-4 minutes, 20% walk away
3. **Margin blindness** — ingredient costs up 15-25%, owners don't know which drinks lose money

**Traditional POS (KiotViet/Sapo):**
- 3-5M hardware upfront
- 200-300K/month software
- Still requires cashier at counter

---

## The Solution

**ScanGo = Self-Service QR Ordering**

```
Customer → Scan QR (3s) → Select items (30s) → Kitchen instantly
= 33 seconds, zero labor, 2% error rate
```

**vs Traditional Flow:**
```
Customer → Queue (2 min) → Order verbally (1 min) 
→ Cashier inputs (30s) → Kitchen
= 3.5-4 minutes, 5M labor, 15% error rate
```

**+ AI profitability insights:** "Trà Đào losing 8K/cup, raise price 5K or switch to oranges"

## Why ScanGo Wins

**4 Differentiation Pillars:**

1. **Self-service ordering** → eliminate cashier (5M/month savings)

**Defensibility:**
- Competitors' POS systems built for staff input, can't retrofit self-service
- AI requires recipe inventory (competitors don't have at budget tier)
- Unit economics only work without hardware margins
