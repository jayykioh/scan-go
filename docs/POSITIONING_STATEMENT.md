**What this means:**

- Human error (15% mistake rate, costly remakes)

### The ScanGo Solution

**How it works:**
1. **Khách tự order:** Quét QR tại bàn → Chọn món → Thanh toán → Bếp nhận ngay (30 giây)
2. **Zero thu ngân:** Không cần người ở quầy, tiết kiệm 5M/tháng
3. **AI báo lời/lỗ:** Mỗi sáng xem món nào đang lỗ vốn, điều chỉnh giá ngay
4. **Tích điểm khách quen:** Khách nhập SĐT, tự động cộng điểm, không cần thẻ

**What you need:**
- Điện thoại/máy tính bất kỳ (bạn đã có)
- In QR code dán bàn (chúng tôi hỗ trợ)
- Kết nối internet (WiFi hoặc 4G)

**What you DON'T need:**

- ❌ Máy in hóa đơn (2 triệu) - tùy chọn
- ❌ Đào tạo phức tạp (setup 10 phút)

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**ScanGo Launches Self-Service Ordering System for Vietnamese Coffee Shops at 99K/Month**

*Đà Nẵng-based startup eliminates cashier bottleneck with QR ordering + AI profitability insights*

**ĐÀ NẴNG, Vietnam (July 12, 2026)** — ScanGo, a new cloud-based ordering platform, today launched its self-service ordering system designed specifically for small coffee and tea shops in Vietnam. Unlike traditional POS systems that cost 3-5 million VND upfront, ScanGo operates entirely through QR codes and costs just 99,000 VND per month with zero hardware requirements.

**The Problem: Labor Costs and Order Mistakes**

Small coffee shops in Vietnam face two major challenges: rising labor costs (cashiers cost 5M VND/month) and high order error rates (15% of orders have mistakes during peak hours, especially for drinks with multiple customizations like bubble tea).

"Every week I see posts in shop owner groups about cashiers quitting suddenly or making too many mistakes," says [Founder name], founder of ScanGo. "And with minimum wage increasing 15% this year, the math is getting harder. I built ScanGo to solve both problems: eliminate the cashier entirely, and let customers input their own order so there are zero mistakes."

**How It Works: 30-Second Self-Service**

Customers scan a QR code at their table, select their drink with all customizations (size, sugar level, ice, toppings), pay via VNPay or cash, and the order appears instantly on the kitchen display. The entire process takes 30 seconds versus 3-4 minutes in traditional cafes.

The system includes an AI-powered profitability tracker that tells shop owners daily which menu items are making money and which are losing money due to ingredient price changes—a feature typically available only in expensive enterprise POS systems.

**Early Results**

During beta testing with 10 coffee shops in Đà Nẵng, ScanGo helped shops reduce order errors from 15% to under 2%, and saved an average of 10.3M VND per month by eliminating the cashier position while maintaining service quality.

"I was skeptical at first," says Hương, owner of Trà Sữa Hương Xưa in Đà Nẵng. "But after one month, customers loved the convenience and I loved seeing real-time which drinks were profitable. The AI even told me my signature drink was losing 8K per cup due to rising lemon prices—I adjusted the price immediately."
