# ScanGo - Strategic Priorities & Execution Roadmap

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-12  
**Planning Horizon:** Q3 2026 - Q2 2027 (12 months)

---

## Executive Summary

**Mission:** Become the default ordering system for 1-15 table coffee and tea shops in Vietnam.

**12-Month Goal:** 1,000 paying customers by Q2 2027

**Revenue Target:** 100M VND MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)

**Strategic Imperative:** Speed. Capture market before KiotViet/Sapo respond with self-service offerings.

---

## Q3 2026 (Jul-Sep): Launch + Validation

### Phase Goal: Prove Product-Market Fit

**Target:** 50 paying customers  
**MRR:** 5M VND  
**Primary Metric:** Customer retention >95% (validates PMF)

### Key Initiatives

#### 1. Product Launch (Weeks 1-4)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Launch Free tier (3 tables, 15 orders/day limit)
- [ ] Launch Lite tier (99K/month, unlimited tables)
- [ ] Landing page with USP messaging
- [ ] Payment integration (VNPay QR)
- [ ] Kitchen Display web app
- [ ] Owner Dashboard (basic reports)

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Can onboard new customer in <1 hour
- Customer can process first order in <5 minutes
- Zero critical bugs in production

**Owner:** Engineering team  
**Budget:** 0đ (pre-built during development)

---

#### 2. Pilot Customer Acquisition (Weeks 5-8)

**Target:** 10 pilot customers in Đà Nẵng

**Approach:**
- Manual outreach (visit shops in person)
- Offer: 3 months free + setup support
- Goal: Deep learning, not revenue

**Selection Criteria:**
- Coffee/tea shop (5-12 tables)
- 0-6 months old (no POS installed yet)
- Owner present daily (can give feedback)
- Located in Đà Nẵng (easy to visit)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Setup guide (step-by-step video)
- [ ] On-site onboarding (first 10 customers)
- [ ] Weekly check-in calls (feedback collection)
- [ ] NFC sticker kit (15 stickers per shop)

**Owner:** Founder  
**Budget:** 5M (travel, NFC stickers, incentives)

**Success Metrics:**
- 10/10 pilots successfully process orders
- 8/10 pilots actively using after 1 month
- Collect 50+ pieces of feedback

---

**Target:** 3 detailed case studies with real numbers

**Format:**

**Distribution:**

**Owner:** Founder (content creation)

**Success Metrics:**
- 3 case studies completed
- Average ROI shown: >50x

- ✅ 50 paying customers acquired

#### 3. Referral Program Launch (Week 16)

**Objective:** Viral growth, reduce CAC

**Mechanics:**
```
Existing customer refers new customer
↓
Both get 1 month free (99K value each)
↓
Referrer gets bonus: 50K credit after referee pays 3 months
```

**Implementation:**
- Unique referral links in dashboard
- Automated email with referral link
- Track referrals in Firestore
- Auto-apply credits

**Marketing:**
- In-app banner: "Giới thiệu bạn, cả 2 được 1 tháng miễn phí"
- Email campaign to existing customers
- WhatsApp broadcast (with permission)

**Owner:** Engineering (2 days) + Founder (promotion)  
**Budget:** 5M (credits given out)

**Success Metrics:**
- 20% of customers generate ≥1 referral
- Viral coefficient: 0.3 (every 3 customers bring 1 new)
- Referral CAC: <100K (vs 400K paid ads)

---

#### 4. Pro Tier Launch (Week 20)

**Objective:** Increase ARPU (Average Revenue Per User), monetize advanced features

**Pro Tier Features (199K/month):**
- ✅ AI Chat (LLM Q&A: "Vì sao Trà Đào lãi ít?")
- ✅ Advanced reports (export Excel, custom date ranges)
- ✅ Split Bill (chia tiền theo người)
- ✅ Multiple template switches (café ↔ trà sữa ↔ nhà hàng)
- ✅ Priority support (response within 4 hours)
- ✅ Offline kit (Android phone server setup guide)

**Pricing Strategy:**
- Keep Lite at 99K (volume tier)
- Pro at 199K (premium tier)
- Free tier unchanged (funnel)

**Upgrade Path:**
- In-app prompts for Pro features
- "Upgrade to see this insight" CTAs
- Email campaign highlighting Pro benefits

**Owner:** Engineering (build) + Founder (marketing)  
**Budget:** 10M (AI API costs projected)

**Success Metrics:**
- 20% of Lite customers upgrade to Pro
- Pro tier ARPU: 199K vs Lite 99K
- Blended ARPU: 120K (80% Lite, 20% Pro)

---

#### 5. Customer Success Hire (Week 18)

**Objective:** Scale support, reduce churn, increase satisfaction

- Handle support tickets (email, chat)

**Compensation:**

**Success Metrics:**
- Average response time <12 hours
- Customer satisfaction: 4.5/5
- Churn reduction: 5% → 3%

## Q1 2027 (Jan-Mar): Moat Building

### Phase Goal: Build Defensibility, Prepare for Competition

**Target:** 500 paying customers (300 net new)  
**MRR:** 50M VND  
**Primary Metric:** LTV/CAC >3x, customer retention >97%

### Key Initiatives

#### 1. Integration Partnerships (Weeks 25-36)

**Objective:** Ecosystem lock-in, increase switching costs

**Priority Integrations:**

**P0 (Must Have):**
- [ ] VNPay (already have) - improve UX
- [ ] Momo payment
- [ ] ZaloPay payment
- [ ] Thermal printer brands (Xprinter, HPRT)

**P1 (Nice to Have):**
- [ ] Accounting software (MISA, Fast)
- [ ] Inventory suppliers (coffee bean distributors)
- [ ] Marketing tools (Zalo OA, Facebook Messenger)

**Partnership Model:**
- Co-marketing (they promote us, we promote them)
- Revenue share (5% of transactions via their platform)
- White-label option (we power their merchant tools)

**Owner:** Founder (BD) + Engineering (integrations)  
**Budget:** 10M (API development, partnership incentives)

**Success Metrics:**
- 3 payment integrations live
- 2 printer partnerships signed
- 40% of transactions via digital payments (vs cash)

---

#### 2. White-Label Offering (Week 28)

**Objective:** Unlock chain customer segment, 10x pricing

**Target Customers:**
- Local chains: 5-20 locations
- Franchise concepts: 3-10 locations
- Food courts: 15-25 vendors

**Offering:**
```
White-Label Package (999K/month or 5M/year):
- Custom domain (order.their-brand.vn)
- Custom branding (colors, logo, fonts)
- Multi-location management dashboard
- Centralized reporting across locations
- Dedicated support (4-hour SLA)
```

**Sales Approach:**
- Outbound: Target known chains (Phúc Long, Highlands alternatives)
- Case study: Pilot with 1 mid-size chain (5 locations)
- Pricing: 999K/month = revenue of 10 regular customers

**Owner:** Founder (sales) + Engineering (multi-tenant features)  
**Budget:** 15M (sales commission, custom development)

**Success Metrics:**
- 5 chain customers signed by end of Q1
- Average deal size: 3M-5M/year
- Chain revenue = 20% of total MRR

---

#### 3. Loyalty Data Platform (Week 30)

**Objective:** Make customer database the moat (hard to switch away)

**Features:**
- Export customer list (CSV, Excel)
- SMS broadcast to loyalty members (via Zalo/SMS gateway)
- Customer segmentation (VIP, regular, lapsed)
- Campaign builder (send offer to specific segments)
- ROI tracking (know which customers are most valuable)

**Value Prop:**
> "Your 500 customer phone numbers = 10M marketing asset. Take it with you if you leave ScanGo (but you won't want to)."

**Pricing:**
- Included in Pro tier (199K)
- Lite tier: Pay per SMS (500đ/message)

**Owner:** Engineering  
**Budget:** 5M (SMS gateway partnership, feature development)

**Success Metrics:**
- 60% of Pro customers use SMS broadcast monthly
- Average broadcast: 200 customers × 3 campaigns/month
- Perceived switching cost: High (customers don't want to lose database)

---

#### 4. AI Insights Expansion (Week 32)

**Objective:** Deepen AI moat, justify Pro tier pricing

**New AI Features:**
- Price optimization: "Tăng giá Trà Đào 5K → doanh thu +800K/tháng"
- Demand forecasting: "Thứ 7 tuần sau cần chuẩn bị 30% more nguyên liệu"
- Customer behavior: "Khách VIP thích order lúc 3-5pm, ưu đãi giờ đó"
- Competitor pricing: Scrape public menus, compare prices

**Technical:**
- Use Gemini API (Google AI)
- Fine-tune on Vietnamese F&B data
- Cost: ~50K/customer/month (acceptable for Pro tier)

**Owner:** Engineering + AI consultant  
**Budget:** 20M (AI development, API costs)

**Success Metrics:**
- 80% of Pro customers use AI weekly
- AI recommendations followed: 40% (proof of value)
- Pro tier churn <2% (AI creates stickiness)

## Q2 2027 (Apr-Jun): Expand or Defend

### Phase Goal: 1,000 Customers OR Defend Market Position

**Target:** 1,000 paying customers (500 net new) OR maintain 500 if defending  
**MRR:** 100M VND  
**Primary Metric:** Market leadership OR competitive survival

### Scenario Planning

This quarter's strategy depends on competitive landscape:

---

### Scenario A: Clear Field (No Major Competitor Response)

**Assumption:** KiotViet/Sapo haven't launched self-service yet

**Strategy:** AGGRESSIVE EXPANSION

#### Initiative A1: Geographic Expansion

**New Cities:**
- Tier 2: Nha Trang, Vũng Tàu, Đà Lạt
- Tier 3: Biên Hòa, Thủ Dầu Một, Long Xuyên

**Approach:**
- Same playbook as Q3/Q4 (Facebook ads, content, referrals)
- Hire local community managers (part-time, 5M/month each)
- City-by-city rollout (focus 1 city per month)

**Budget:** 50M/quarter (ads + community managers)

---

#### Initiative A2: Vertical Expansion

**New Segments:**
- Street food formalization (government program)
- Food courts (multi-tenant architecture)
- Bakeries (similar to coffee shops)

**Approach:**
- Dedicated templates for each vertical
- Vertical-specific case studies
- Partnership with industry associations

**Budget:** 20M/quarter (template development, BD)

---

#### Initiative A3: Price Increase

**Rationale:**
- Market position established
- Competitors still higher-priced
- Can increase without losing customers

**New Pricing:**
- Free: Unchanged
- Lite: 99K → 129K (+30%, grandfathered existing)
- Pro: 199K → 249K (+25%, grandfathered existing)

**Impact:**
- New customer ARPU: 140K (vs 120K current)
- MRR boost: +15M from price alone

---

### Scenario B: Competitors Respond (KiotViet Launches Self-Service)

**Assumption:** Major competitor enters self-service market

**Strategy:** DEFENSIVE CONSOLIDATION

#### Initiative B1: Customer Retention Blitz

**Tactics:**
- Lock-in contracts: Pay annually, get 2 months free (10M upfront vs 12M monthly)
- Loyalty bonuses: Stay 12 months, get free hardware (NFC kit)
- Price freeze: Guarantee no price increases for 2 years

**Goal:** Lock in existing customers before competitor poaches

**Budget:** 20M (incentives, discounts)

---

#### Initiative B2: AI Differentiation Campaign

**Messaging:**
> "KiotViet added self-service. We've been self-service for 2 years. That's why our AI is better—we have the data."

**Tactics:**
- Emphasize AI advantage (they don't have at budget tier)
- Show data: "2 years of Vietnamese coffee shop data"
- Case studies: AI saved customers X million

**Budget:** 10M (content, ads highlighting AI)

---

#### Initiative B3: Feature Velocity War

**Strategy:** Ship features weekly, stay 6 months ahead

**High-impact features:**
- Voice ordering (Vietnamese speech recognition)
- Instagram Story integration
- Automatic social media posting
- Customer analytics dashboard
- Supply chain recommendations

**Goal:** Move too fast for competitors to keep up

**Budget:** 30M (engineering sprint, contract developers)

---

#### Initiative B4: Strategic Partnerships

**Defensive moats:**
- Exclusive partnership with VNPay (preferred merchant POS)
- Partnership with coffee bean suppliers (bundled offering)
- Integration with accounting software (ecosystem lock-in)

**Goal:** Make it hard to switch (customers lose integrations)

**Budget:** 15M (partnership incentives)

---

### Q2 Success Criteria

**Scenario A (Expansion):**
- ✅ 1,000 customers reached
- ✅ 100M MRR achieved
- ✅ 3 new cities launched
- ✅ 1 new vertical proven (>50 customers)

**Scenario B (Defense):**
- ✅ Maintain 500+ customers (churn <2%)
- ✅ 80% of customers on annual contracts
- ✅ AI usage at 80% (differentiation proven)
- ✅ Feature releases: 12+ in quarter (weekly velocity)

**Red Flags (Either Scenario):**
- ❌ Churn >5% = competitive threat or product decline
- ❌ CAC >1M = market saturation or ad inefficiency
- ❌ No new customer signups = market tapped out

## Risk Management

### Critical Assumptions to Validate

**Assumption 1:** Coffee shops will adopt QR self-service
- **Test:** Q3 pilot results
- **Kill criterion:** <50% adoption in pilots

**Assumption 2:** 99K pricing is sustainable
- **Test:** Unit economics after 100 customers
- **Kill criterion:** Gross margin <60%

**Assumption 3:** CAC <500K is achievable at scale
- **Test:** Q4 Facebook ads
- **Kill criterion:** CAC >800K for 3 consecutive months

**Assumption 4:** Self-service eliminates cashier (not just reduces)
- **Test:** Customer testimonials
- **Kill criterion:** Customers still hiring cashiers after 6 months

---

### Contingency Plans

**If churn >5%:**
1. Immediate customer interviews (why leaving?)
2. Feature gap analysis
3. Win-back campaign (1 month free to return)
4. If systemic: Pivot or pause growth

**If CAC >1M:**
1. Pause paid ads
2. Double down on referrals + organic
3. Reassess target market (maybe wrong segment)
4. Consider B2B sales (chains) vs B2C (individual shops)

**If competitor launches:**
1. Execute Scenario B (defend)
2. Emphasize AI differentiation
3. Lock customers with annual contracts
4. Accelerate feature velocity
